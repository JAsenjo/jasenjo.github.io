Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026
App: Mini Games Club
Privacy contact: minigameschallenge@gmail.com

This document explains clearly how we process data in Mini Games Club to meet typical Google Play and App Store requirements. It does not replace professional legal advice. If the app’s practices change (for example, new SDKs), we will update this page.

1. Who we are

Mini Games Club is a mobile online mini-games app with ranking, available for Android and iOS. The controller of the data described in this policy is the app developer, reachable at minigameschallenge@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy applies to the use of Mini Games Club on mobile devices (Android and iOS), including guest access, accounts linked to Google or Apple, ads, and in-app purchases (one-time payments managed by Google Play and the App Store).

3. Data we may process

Depending on how you use the app, we may process the following categories:

3.1 Account and player identity

  • Firebase Authentication user identifier (UID).
  • Session type: guest (anonymous) or linked account.
  • Nick / player name you choose.
  • Country or region you select for ranking (country code and associated flag).
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple: identifiers and basic data those providers share to authenticate you (for example provider ID and, where applicable, display name or email, as authorized by the provider and you).
  • Profile creation/update timestamps.

3.2 Technical and device data

  • Basic device and OS information needed for the app and services (for example Android/iOS platform).
  • Technical identifiers related to authentication, security, and abuse prevention (via Firebase Authentication and sign-in providers).
  • Local app preferences (for example language and whether audio is on), stored on the device.

3.3 In-app purchases (one-time)

The app may offer one-time in-app purchases (not auto-renewing subscriptions) through:

  • Google Play Billing on Android.
  • Apple In-App Purchase / StoreKit on iOS.

Charging, invoices, payment methods, and most purchase data are handled by Google or Apple, not directly by us. In connection with those purchases we may process or receive, depending on platform and product:

  • Product / purchase identifiers (product ID, transaction ID, purchase token or equivalents).
  • Purchase status (purchased, restored, pending, refunded when the store notifies us).
  • Purchase platform (Android / iOS) and, where applicable, association with your app UID to activate the purchased benefit (for example unlock or ad removal).
  • Approximate transaction date/time for validation and support.

We do not receive or store your full card number or complete bank details: those are processed by Google Play or the App Store under their policies. For refunds and payment management you must use the corresponding store channels, without prejudice to contacting us for technical support of the in-app benefit.

More information: Google Privacy, Apple Privacy.

3.4 Ads

The app may show third-party ads (for example ad networks such as Google AdMob or equivalents). Ad providers may collect or receive data such as:

  • Device advertising identifiers (AAID / IDFA or equivalents), subject to OS permissions and controls.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad type).
  • Approximate information derived from IP or other technical data used by the ad network to serve, measure, and where applicable personalize ads.
  • Ad SDK diagnostic or performance data.

The specific processing of this data is done by ad providers under their own policies. You may consult, among others: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob / Google Mobile Ads privacy documentation.

If an in-app purchase removes or reduces ads, we will stop showing (or limit) advertising associated with that benefit as far as technically possible on your account/device.

3.5 Data we do not deliberately request

  • We do not intentionally request special sensitive data (health, religion, etc.).
  • We do not request precise GPS location for normal gameplay.
  • We do not sell your contacts list or access your gallery or microphone for normal app use.

4. Purposes of processing

  • Create and maintain your account or guest session.
  • Show your nick, country, and results in online rankings.
  • Allow linking a guest account to Google or Apple without losing progress tied to the same UID.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the app.
  • Handle support requests sent by email.
  • Show ads to fund the app and measure their performance.
  • Process and validate one-time in-app purchases, activate the purchased benefit, and allow restoration of non-consumable purchases when applicable.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to valid authority requests.

5. Legal bases

We process data because it is necessary to provide the service you request (account, profile, ranking, support, delivery of purchased content/benefit), for our legitimate interest in securing and improving the app and monetizing it with ads and in-app purchases in a proportionate way, and, where applicable, because you give consent (for example OS privacy/ads consents or the ads SDK, such as ATT on iOS or consent forms when they apply). The purchase itself is completed with Google or Apple under the store’s terms.

6. Processors and third parties

We may use providers that process data on our behalf or independently for:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and related services).
  • Google Sign-In / Apple Sign In for authentication.
  • Google Play (in-app purchase billing on Android).
  • Apple App Store (in-app purchases on iOS).
  • Ad networks (e.g. Google AdMob or others we indicate in updates).
  • Hosting infrastructure for this policy and the app on official stores.

These third parties may process data in different countries. Where applicable, recognized transfer mechanisms under applicable law apply.

7. Retention

  • Profile and account data are kept while you keep the account or as needed for ranking and the service.
  • Local preferences remain on the device until you clear them or uninstall the app.
  • Ad providers, stores, and Firebase may retain certain technical or transaction data under their policies and legal/tax obligations.
  • Purchase records associated with your in-app account are kept as long as needed to provide the purchased benefit, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.
  • If you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize personal data we control, unless we must retain it for legal obligations (e.g. transaction records required by law or the store).

8. Your rights and account deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example GDPR in the EEA/UK or other applicable laws), you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, or portability, where applicable.

Account / data deletion: email minigameschallenge@gmail.com with the subject Mini Games Club Account Deletion and tell us the platform (Android/iOS) and the nick or details that identify your account. We will handle the request within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law and store requirements.

You can also sign out from Settings. Note that an unlinked guest session may be lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

9. Ads and device privacy controls

  • On Android you can limit personalized ads from Google / device privacy settings.
  • On iOS you can manage tracking (ATT) and system privacy permissions.
  • Rejecting or limiting certain consents may result in less personalized ads, but does not necessarily mean no ads at all.

10. Children

Mini Games Club is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us to delete it.

If in the future the app targets children or “Designed for Families” / equivalent categories, we will update this policy and ad configuration accordingly.

11. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures (including Google/Firebase services with encryption in transit) to protect data. No system is 100 % secure; please protect your device and your Google/Apple accounts.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will reflect the change. Continued use of the app after relevant changes are published means you have had the opportunity to review the current version at this public URL.

13. Contact

Privacy and rights: minigameschallenge@gmail.com
General support from the app (Settings → Help) or the same email.

Para reembolsos y gestión de pagos debes usar los canales de la tienda correspondiente, sin perjuicio de contactarnos para soporte técnico del beneficio en la app.
